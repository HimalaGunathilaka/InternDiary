# YOLO model size comparison
- **n (Nano):** Extremely light and fast, designed for edge devices with very limited resources.
- **s (Small):** Balances speed and accuracy; generally good for real-time applications on modest hardware.
- **m (Medium):** General-purpose model, offering a higher accuracy than 's' while maintaining decent speed.
- **l (Large):** High accuracy, suitable for desktop GPUs or backend servers, but with higher latency.
- **x (Extra Large):** Maximum accuracy, designed for high-end hardware, usually the slowest.

# EasyOCR vs Mistal

|**Feature**|**EasyOCR**|**Mistral OCR (v3 / 2512)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Category**|Traditional (CRAFT + CRNN)|LLM-based (Multimodal)|
|**Deployment**|**Local** (Runs on your PC/GPU)|**Cloud API** (Requires Internet)|
|**Latency**|Fast (~50–200ms per crop)|Slower (~1–3 seconds per request)|
|**Cost**|Free (Open Source)|Paid ($1–$2 per 1,000 pages)|
|**Context**|Reads only what it "sees"|Understands structure and context|
|**Best For**|Real-time traffic feeds|High-accuracy post-processing|
