# [LD2450 sensor](https://esphome.io/components/sensor/ld2450/)  24 GHz
![[Pasted image 20260427125226.png]]
- And 200mA power input. Baud rate 256000.
- The serial port output data contains the location and speed information of up to three targets.
- It has radar sensor and intelligent algorithm firmware. Primarily intended for use indoor to enable the location tracking of moving human targets.
- https://youtu.be/-s4deQ5IfwQ?si=v4PO41-PJg5xwxa1
- Can detect the target when not moving as well.
- Hang it about chest height.
- Not enough to detect heart beat and respiration.


> [!note]
> - 60 GHz is free to use because its within the attenuation band of oxygen. Therefor signal strength tends to drops a lot.
