# [DHT22](https://learn.adafruit.com/dht/overview)
- 3V~5V power
- 2.5mA max current during conversion
- Relative humidity with 2~5% accuracy
- $-40^{o}C$ to $80^{o}C$ temperature reading with $\pm 0.5^{o}C$ accuracy
- Sampling rate 0.5Hz (Every two seconds)
- Size 15.1mm x 25mm x 7.7mm
