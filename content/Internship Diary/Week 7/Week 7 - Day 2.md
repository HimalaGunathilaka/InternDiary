# Chrome webstore requirements for publishing extension
- $5 fee
- 128 by 128 icon image
- Promo video
- Screen shots (Resolution of them will be provided in the form)
- Provide a crisp and clear description (Chrome people check it clearly)
- Why use these permission , need to be explained.
- Payment method (free or not)

### [In there privacy and policies for developers](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/terms)
