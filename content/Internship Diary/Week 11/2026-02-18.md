# [Otsu's Thresholding](https://learnopencv.com/otsu-thresholding-with-opencv/) 
- **Image Thresholding** ---> Is used to binarize the image based on pixel intensities. (Output most  of the time is a grayscale image.)
- Here the basic functionality is dynamicaly adjust the threshold value in such a way to preserve the real world information.
- **Image segmentation** ---> Partition the image into different segments or groups of pixels. (In that sense Image Thresholding is the simplest one since it divide the pixels into two groups.)
